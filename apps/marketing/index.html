What it does

Five things, done properly.

01

Orders

A guest scans a QR at the table, or a waiter takes it on their own screen. Either way, it's in the kitchen in seconds, no relaying by hand.

02

Kitchen

One screen per station — kitchen and bar see only their own tickets, in the order they were fired. Nothing shouted across the pass.

03

Billing

Tax, discounts, split tender, cash drawer reconciliation — the whole bill, closed correctly, every time, on every table.

04

Invoicing

GSTIN on file, a sequential invoice number that resets on the financial year, printed on real 80mm thermal paper — not a hand-written slip.

05

Reporting

What sold, what came in, and what's still outstanding — at the end of every single night, without adding up a cashbook by hand.
